OpenADB extracted and compacted the 24 px, rounded, weight-400, unfilled vector
path data, then recolors and renders it at runtime to support light, dark,
system, disabled, active, and selected states. These formatting and color
changes are OpenADB modifications. The OpenADB logo and Android
application-provided icons are not part of Material Symbols and are not
modified by this icon layer.
